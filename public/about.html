Our story

Built by a Muslim,
for everyone
who needs to pray.

Fassah started with one problem: being somewhere new, Asr time closing in, and no idea where to go. That should never happen.

I was in central London, a meeting ran over, and I realised I had 20 minutes before Asr would pass. I had no idea where the nearest prayer space was. I missed it.

Y
Yaasir — Founder
CS Student, Brunel University · London

Why Fassah exists.

That moment wasn't unique. 3.5 million Muslims live in the UK. Every single one of them needs to find somewhere to pray, five times a day, wherever life takes them.

The information exists — mosques, university prayer rooms, quiet outdoor corners, business prayer spaces. But it's scattered across Google Maps, WhatsApp groups, word of mouth, and personal memory. There's no single place to find it all.

Fassah is that place. Community-built. Free forever. No account required. No ads. Just a map that helps Muslims never miss Salah because they didn't know where to go.

And it's not just for Muslims — anyone who needs a quiet, respectful space to pause and reflect can use it.
